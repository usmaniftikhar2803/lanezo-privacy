Lanezo – Privacy Policy

Turbo Motorway Racing • Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Lanezo is a 3D motorway racing game for Android, designed and developed by Hafiz Muhammad Usman, Web & App Developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game uses and how it is handled. Your privacy matters, and Lanezo is built to respect it.

2. Information We Collect

Lanezo does not run its own servers and does not collect personal information. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal detail to play.

The only optional exception is the Google Play Games leaderboard (see section 4). If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your score is sent to Google Play Games Services so that you can appear on the leaderboard.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data locally on your own device only:

  • Your best score and total coins
  • Your daily-reward streak and mission progress
  • The cars you unlocked and your car customizations (paint, rims, stickers, body kits)
  • Your settings (sound, music, camera view, weather/time)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

4. Google Play Games Services (leaderboard)

Lanezo can connect to Google Play Games Services, a service provided by Google, to show a leaderboard so you can compare your score with friends and other players. When you are signed in to Google Play Games, the following is processed by Google: your Play Games player ID, your public Play Games name and profile picture, and the scores you submit. This information is handled under Google's Privacy Policy, and Lanezo does not receive your email address or any other personal detail. If you do not want to use the leaderboard, simply do not sign in to Google Play Games; the game works the same without it. You can manage or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app.

5. Advertising and Analytics

Lanezo does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, and it does not sell your data. Apart from the optional Google Play Games leaderboard above, the game runs offline using files bundled inside the app.

6. Permissions

The app uses the Android Internet permission only so that the game can run inside the app and so that buttons such as Rate this game, Share with friends and Privacy Policy can open Google Play, the share sheet or this page. No personal data is sent through this permission. The Vibrate permission is used only to give short haptic feedback during the game (it can be turned off in Settings). Lanezo does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or storage.

7. Children's Privacy

Lanezo is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Google Play Games sign-in is optional and is subject to Google's own age requirements.

8. Data Security

Your game data stays on your device. The only data that leaves it is the leaderboard score you submit through Google Play Games. Keeping your device secure keeps your game progress secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time, for example when new features are added. Any changes will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
