Lanezo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lanezo (the “game”, “app”), published by Calvo (“we”, “us”), handles information. Lanezo is a 3D motorway racing game for Android (package name com.calvo.lanezo). By installing and playing the game you agree to this policy.

In short: Lanezo does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data. Your game progress stays on your own device. If the game shows ads, those are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect limited technical data as described in Section 4.

1. Information we do not collect

  • No account, login or registration is needed to play.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or home address.
  • We do not access your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone.
  • We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not store any personal data about you.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following data only on your device (in the app’s private storage). It is never sent to us:

DataWhy it is saved
Best score, coins and unlocked carsSo your progress is still there the next time you play.
Car customization (paint, rims, stickers, body kits)To show your chosen look.
Settings (sound, music, weather, camera view)To remember your preferences.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the app’s data in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Lanezo → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the game.

3. Permissions

  • Internet access – used so the app can run its built-in game screen and, if ads are shown, to load ads. The game itself works without an internet connection.

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Lanezo is free and may show advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and use information such as:

  • Your device’s advertising ID (a resettable identifier);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and software information (model, operating system, language);
  • App interaction and diagnostic data (for example app launches, ad views and clicks, crashes and performance).

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive or control this data. To learn how Google handles information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area or the UK), ad personalization depends on the consent choice you make.

5. Links and actions that leave the game

The game’s Settings menu has buttons that open other apps or websites only when you tap them:

  • Rate this game opens the Google Play Store.
  • Share with friends opens Android’s share sheet. You choose where the message goes.
  • Feedback / Contact opens your email app. If you send us an email, we receive the email address and the message you choose to send, and we use them only to reply to you.
  • Privacy policy opens this page in your browser.

These services are run by third parties and have their own privacy policies.

6. Children’s privacy

Lanezo is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices

  • Ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads) or at adssettings.google.com.
  • Game data: clear the app’s data or uninstall the game to remove everything saved on your device.
  • Questions or requests: contact us at the email address below.

8. Data security and retention

Because your game data stays on your device and we do not operate a game server, we do not keep any personal data about you. Your saved progress remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the “Last updated” date at the top. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
